What has been happening

- The programme has four tracks: MIT Universal AI (self-paced online course), Wadhwani Ignite (14-week entrepreneurship programme with live sessions), later advanced training with the Ethiopian AI Institute, and an in-person bootcamp in Addis Ababa for selected teams.
